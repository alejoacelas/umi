Pane indicator

One small colour-coded mark per pane, in place of the cyan focus line and the agent marks. It says whether a pane is working, needs you, finished while you weren't looking, or is idle, and it also shows which pane has focus. The same mark appears on the pane's chip in the strip, and a tab's sidebar row shows the mark of its most urgent pane. Each section changes one thing; everything else stays at the stated default. Content is made up. See also: Where panes live · Pane strip · Resurfacing · Drawer · Keyboard moves.
