Where a pane can be, and how a hidden one shows

A hidden pane stays in its tab with its program running, out of the layout. These mocks compare five ways to show that it exists, and what happens when a hidden agent ends its turn without Status: done. Commands and config lines are illustrative.

Hidden

Out of the layout, still in its tab, still running. Can come back by itself.

umi pane hide p3 \
  --until 'agent.done=false'
