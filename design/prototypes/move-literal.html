Moving panes in place

While you move a pane, the window shows the tab it would land in, at full size, exactly as it would look afterwards: the moving pane in its spot, and the pane that would be hidden if the tab already shows three. The sidebar comes out for the move even if it was hidden, and goes away again after. The question these options answer differently: how do you know at a glance which tab, and which panes, you are looking at? Everything else is the same in all four. The focused pane, q4-notes, came here from impact-ops (main). Two tabs share the folder impact-ops, so names alone don't tell them apart. Syntax is illustrative, content is made up. What the sidebar says today, for comparison. Each row is the tab's folder (or the name you gave it) and branch, then line 2: the agent's title or the last command. It never lists a tab's panes, so "impact-ops main" and "impact-ops billing" read almost alike, and a tab's second and third panes don't appear at all. That is the gap each option fills during a move.
