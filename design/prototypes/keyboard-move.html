Moving panes from the keyboard

Three ways to move the focused pane to another tab without the mouse, each showing what will happen before it happens. In every option Enter moves it and you stay where you are, ⇧Enter moves it and follows it, Esc puts everything back. The focused pane, q4-notes, came here from impact-ops, so impact-ops is marked "back". Today ⌃⌘← / ⌃⌘→ moves a pane to the previous or next tab at once, with no preview. Syntax is illustrative, content is made up. Related: Where panes live · Resurfacing · The drawer.
